GENERAL INFORMATION

What Your Path actually is.

Your Path is an AI-powered career exploration platform designed for students. Instead of matching you to a job title with a static quiz, it asks adaptive questions about your genuine interests, working style, motivation and the pressure around you — then explains the reasoning behind every pathway it suggests.

It is decision support, not a verdict. There is no “97% career match” here. Every pathway comes with its skills, education routes, trade-offs, alternatives, and one small real-world experiment you can run this month.

General Information

Your Path is an AI-powered career exploration platform for students. Adaptive questioning surfaces the interests, working styles and motivations that actually appear in your answers — then turns them into pathways you can test in the real world.

Every session asks 20 questions across ten dimensions, from genuine interests to external pressure. Selection weights describe how questions are chosen; they are never a personality score.

Is YourPath safe to use?

Short answer: yes. This prototype stores everything in your own browser's local storage — your profile, answers and saved pathways never leave your device, and no server receives them.

  • No third-party trackers, advertising pixels, or resale of student data.
  • No AI provider keys or student records are exposed in the frontend.
  • You can clear everything at any time from Cookie settings in the footer, or by clearing this site's browser storage.
  • Pathways are explained as hypotheses to test, never as diagnoses or guarantees.

For a real multi-user deployment the same data would be protected with server-side sessions, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, and documented retention and deletion rules.

FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers about how Your Path works, what it stores, and what it will never claim to know.

Does Your Path tell me which career to choose?

No. It shows several plausible directions with their reasoning, trade-offs and alternatives, plus one small experiment for each. The decision stays yours — and “I don’t know yet” is a normal, honest answer.

Where is my data stored?

In this prototype, entirely in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing this site’s storage removes your profile, answers, saved pathways and preferences.

Can one questionnaire decide my future?

No, and any platform that claims otherwise is overselling. A 20-question session is one sample of evidence. Retaking it later and running real experiments gives better information.

How do I delete my account or my answers?

Log out, then clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or use Cookie settings in the footer. A production deployment would also provide an in-app deletion request flow.

Is this a replacement for a school counselor?

No. Your Path is decision support. Teachers, counselors, parents and professionals should stay part of the conversation.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@yourpath.demo or post in the Discord community. Include the page you were on and what you expected to happen.

PRIVACY POLICY

How Your Path handles your information.

everything you enter stays on your own device. This notice explains exactly what that means, and what a production deployment must add before real students use it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details you type in: name, email, optional phone, country, grade, age and optional school.
  • Questionnaire answers, saved pathways, comparison choices and feedback you submit.
  • Preferences such as your cookie choice and login state.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, camera or microphone access, or advertising identifiers.

2. Where it is stored

All of the above is written to your browser's local storage under keys prefixed yp_. Nothing is transmitted to a server, and no third party receives it. If you clear this site's data, everything listed above is permanently removed.

3. What we never do

  • No selling, renting or sharing of student data.
  • No third-party advertising or cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No AI provider keys, secrets or server credentials in the frontend.
  • No presenting a questionnaire result as a diagnosis or a guaranteed outcome.

4. Your rights and controls

  • Access — your data is visible in the Profile and Dashboard screens at any time.
  • Correction — edit your profile from Dashboard → Profile and save.
  • Deletion — use Cookie settings, or clear this site's storage in your browser.
  • Withdrawal of consent — log out; no further data is collected until you sign in again.

5. Students and age

Your Path is intended for students aged 13 and over. Where a country requires guardian consent, that requirement must be implemented before launch. Do not enter sensitive personal, medical or family information into this demo.

6. Before a real launch

  • Use server-side sessions or a vetted authentication provider — never browser-only auth.
  • Encrypt data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest; minimize what is collected in the first place.
  • Use role-based access control and audit logs for any staff access.
  • Implement age and guardian consent rules for every country served.
  • Document retention, correction and deletion procedures, and honour deletion requests.
  • Keep AI provider keys server-side and document the model provider's data-retention policy.
  • Use aggregated analytics rather than exposing identifiable student records.
  • Have student-facing guidance reviewed by qualified humans before publication.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@yourpath.demo. See the for how the service may be used.
